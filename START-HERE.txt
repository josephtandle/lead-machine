THE LEAD MACHINE - START HERE

This folder finds you 25 real leads who fit your business. No email needed,
no signup, no guessed contact info. You approve and send everything yourself.

TWO WAYS TO RUN IT

WAY 1: MOST PEOPLE (2 minutes, no install)
1. Open PASTE-INTO-CHATGPT-OR-CLAUDE.txt in this folder.
2. Select all the text and copy it.
3. Open ChatGPT or Claude in your browser, start a new chat, paste it in, send.
4. It asks: cold outreach, warm network, or both. Say "cold" (or "both").
   If it cannot work out your business from the chat, answer its one
   question or paste your website link. You get 25 leads back in the chat.
5. Say "more" any time for another fresh 25, no repeats.

WAY 2: IF YOU USE CLAUDE CODE, CODEX, OR GEMINI CLI
1. Open this whole folder in that tool.
2. Say "go".
3. It asks cold, warm, or both, figures out your business from this folder
   if it can, asks one question if it can't, then hands you 25 leads.
4. It remembers everyone you have already been shown, across days, so "more"
   never repeats a name.

WHAT IT NEVER DOES

It never sends a message, an email, or a DM on its own. It only ever finds
people and shows you how to reach them yourself. You read every name, you
decide, you send it yourself from your own accounts.

If a terminal is not your thing, just use Way 1. Both ways do the same job.
