Interview to fill in whatever MY-BUSINESS.md is missing for advanced mode. But
first: if you can already see who this person is (they pasted their website,
LinkedIn About text, a bio, or MY-BUSINESS.md already has real content), say
so, DRAFT the missing answers yourself, show the draft, and only ask about
what you could not fill in. Never ask them to repeat something you already
know.

Otherwise ask one question at a time, in plain language, and wait for each
answer:
1. What do you sell, and what does it cost?
2. Who buys it? Describe your best customer in one sentence.
3. What problem does it solve for them, in their words if you can?
4. Which channels do you actually use to talk to people (LinkedIn, WhatsApp,
   Instagram, email)?
5. Paste 3 short texts you recently sent a friend, so the voice can be learned.
6. Who are your existing clients? (names, emails, or phone numbers are enough)
7. Who must never be contacted besides clients? (family, close friends, or a
   rough list is fine)

Then:
- Update MY-BUSINESS.md with 1-5, including the voice samples verbatim.
- Create data/my-clients.csv, data/do-not-contact.csv, and
  data/already-messaged.csv from the headers in templates/, if they do not
  already exist.
- Put the answer to 6 into data/my-clients.csv and the answer to 7 into
  data/do-not-contact.csv.

Finish by stating the daily loop: hunt N -> screen -> draft -> the person
approves and sends by hand.
